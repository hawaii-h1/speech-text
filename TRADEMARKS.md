## What you may do

- Say what your work is: "a fork of SpeechText", "based on SpeechText", "works with SpeechText". That is what a name is for.
- Write about SpeechText, review it, teach it, link to it, and use its name and icon to do so.
- Build the unmodified source and run it yourself, under its own name.
- Redistribute an unmodified release, as published, under its own name.

## What needs another name

A modified version you distribute — a fork, a rebuild with changes, a paid edition, a hosted service built on it — needs a name and an icon of its own that cannot be mistaken for SpeechText's. Change the product name, the icon and the bundle identifier (`app.speech-text.SpeechText` is this project's, and macOS keys its users' permission grants to it). Keep the copyright notices and the licence, as the GPL requires; the name is the only thing to change.

Do not present your work as SpeechText, as made or endorsed by this project, or as an official build — in its name, its domain, its listing in an app store, or its marketing.

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, [open an issue](https://github.com/yavno/speech-text/issues) and ask.
