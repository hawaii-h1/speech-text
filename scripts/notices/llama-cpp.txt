llama.cpp — llama.framework (build b11696)
The engine AI Rewrite runs a model on this Mac with: llama.cpp and ggml by the ggml
authors (https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp), as the prebuilt llama.xcframework of
release b11696 (commit ceefd16da), inside the SpeechTextLLM service. Published under the
MIT License. The models it runs are downloaded by the user from HuggingFace and are not
part of this app.
